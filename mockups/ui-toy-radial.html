Jump to a state

Everything is live. Tap a blob, clay, grass, the stream, the home nest. Drag across clay or water to paint, drag grass to look around, wheel to zoom. Keys: Space pause, C chatter, U the bunch, Esc backs out.

Direction: Toybox, with the radial tool

Toybox's look and top bar, unchanged: cream plates, dark ink, Fredoka, 56 px buttons with a toy lip, 3 text sizes, the stock plate, pause / speed / bunch / menu and the alert pills. The bottom tool tray and its modes are gone. Instead, Radial's way of playing: tap a thing and a ring of only what makes sense there opens around it, as Toybox buttons with label plates. Drag across clay to mark digging, drag across a stream for a bridge, drag grass to pan. The tapped square is easy to see: the meadow dims around it, it gets a thick sun outline that breathes, and hovering or pressing a build button shows a see-through copy of it there.

Morphis having fun

  • Every few seconds a couple of idle blobs take a little break: they bump into each other and have a chat, play tag, roll a ball back and forth, bounce together, splash in the stream, nap in the nest, or toss a ball to Pip (Pip's fun goes up a bit).
  • Speech bubbles are cream plates with ink text at 16 px, wrapped to at most 3 lines, never cut, kept on screen and clear of the top bar. One bubble at a time in a pair, so they never stack.
  • A blob that is picked stops where it is; its friend may keep chatting to it.

Chatter

  • One tap on the speech-bubble button. It is a panel, not a pop-up: the meadow keeps playing beside it (desktop: right side; phone: the bottom half), and new lines slide in at the bottom as bubbles appear. A berry badge counts lines you have not seen.
  • Each row: the blob's colour face, name, where and when, and the line. Tap a row: the camera goes to that blob and it waves.
  • A picked blob's caption shows its last two lines under "Lately".

Baby off screen

When Pip needs something and is off screen (or under the top bar), a 56 px cream disc with Pip's face and a berry need dot sits on the screen edge, an ink arrow pointing at Pip. It stays below the top bar and the alert pills and clear of the Chatter panel. Tap it: the camera goes to Pip and the care ring opens.
