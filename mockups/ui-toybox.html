What is wrong with the current UI

  1. Too many text sizes, picked per string. Two font sets of five sizes each (desk 10/12/16/26/46, touch 12/14/18/28/44) at src/ui.lua:44-47. Then almost every label goes through a fallback chain {hud, small, tiny} and takes the first size that fits (printLine / printFit, src/ui.lua:375-430; tool labels src/ui.lua:1043-1055), so labels of the same kind sit side by side in different sizes.
  2. Text gets tiny, then cut off. The whole HUD is drawn at a fractional scale clamped to 0.6-3.0 (src/ui.lua:131-143), so the 10 px face can land near 6 px. The C64 face (src/ui.lua:16) is very wide, so strings get cut: at 1280x720 the tray reads "2 stick..", "1 stick..", and the hint "Click a morphi with SELECT to see what i..".
  3. About a dozen button sizes. Top bar 28/34 tall (src/ui.lua:149); tools 60/64 tall and 56-120 wide (src/ui.lua:157,186); FOLLOW 76x22 / JOBS 56x22 (src/ui.lua:1154-1155); the narrow strip's JOBS 64x20 and FOLLOW 70x20 (src/ui.lua:1222,1225); panel rows 40 (src/ui.lua:243); tama buttons 34/40 (src/ui.lua:1461); job grid 38, then 30, then 26 (src/ui.lua:1628,1671-1672); MEADOW 110x32, CLOSE 96x30 (src/ui.lua:1426,1550). A 20 px button is half a fingertip.
  4. Text straight over the meadow. Alerts sit on 65% black (src/ui.lua:991), the toast on 75% (src/ui.lua:1240), the narrow morphi strip on 60% (src/ui.lua:1216), with red text (C.warn) over a busy, bright, moving map.
  5. The top bar folds at an ordinary desktop size. At 1280x720 the stock collapses into one "HUNGRY 0" dropdown and ten buttons hide under a gear (src/ui.lua:735-736). Reading your sticks takes a tap, and SPEED is gear, then SPEED.
  6. Picking a morphi needs the SELECT tool. With any other tool a tap on the map acts (main.lua:1684-1700), so after digging you go back to SELECT first. Even the J key answers "Select a morphi first (SELECT tool, then tap one)" (main.lua:1226).
  7. Jobs are a modal form. 13 buttons, three explanation sentences, UNITS and CLOSE (src/ui.lua:1621-1730). FORAGER shows in red in the neutral row with no reason until you tap it.
  8. Baby care is two stacked full screens. TEND opens the tama panel (src/ui.lua:1414), FOOD opens the food bag over it (src/ui.lua:1524), then MEADOW to get back.
  9. Banned word in the voice. The menu asks "Quit to the title screen?" with a QUIT button (src/ui.lua:2422-2424). "quit" is on the CLAUDE.md banned list.
  10. Things on screen push each other around. The pause banner, restart call, goal box, alerts, follow arrows and toast each place themselves against the others (bannerBottom / restartBottom, src/ui.lua:1747-1790, 938-955), so the layout changes with whatever happens to be showing.
  11. Phone portrait wastes the tray. Four tools per row (src/ui.lua:165) leaves a half-empty second row for 6 tools, plus a hint block in two sizes.
