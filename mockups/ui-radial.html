How it plays

You never steer a morphi. Tap anything and pick from the ring around it.

Drag across clay to mark digging. Drag along a stream for a bridge. Drag grass to look around.

Tap a job on a morphi for its main job; hold it (or right click) for the one it does next.

Design notes

Direction: Direct / Radial. The meadow is the interface. No tool bar, no top bar: tap a thing, a ring of big buttons blooms around it with only what makes sense there. One action is one tap once the ring is open. Painting (clay, bridges) is a drag that starts on the thing itself, so there are no modes to leave.

Size and type scale (tokens)
--font Baloo 2, one rounded face (the C64 face is too wide, which is what forces today's shrinking)
--fs-label 15px / 700: every label, number, caption line, toast, plate. Nothing is ever smaller, nothing is ever cut with "..".
--fs-title 20px / 800: sheet titles and the name line in the caption. That is the whole type scale: 2 sizes.
--hit 60px ring buttons (56 on phones), --hit-center 52px, --hit-min 44px for pill, chips, sheet buttons.
--s1..s5 4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 24 spacing. --r-plate 10, --r-card 16, round 999.
Plates: solid #1d2819 at 94% under every label, ink #fbf8ec (contrast ~15:1), dim #c9d1b2.
States: main sun ring + "1", then-job leaf ring + "2", grumbles berry.
Ring radius: 78px for 1-3 buttons, 88 for 4-5, 104 for 6+. Bloom 170ms, 20ms stagger. Hold = 450ms with a filling sun ring.
Problems in the current UI
  1. Ten font sizes, then shrink-to-fit. Two font sets of five (src/ui.lua:44-47), and printFit / printLine / printWrap (ui.lua:375-430) drop each string to the 10px face or cut it with "..". In the live build: "2 stick..", "1-6...", "the Pupper, e..", "pinch to..".
  2. The face is too wide for the boxes. The C64 font (ui.lua:16) is what forces the fitting, and the HUD scale can clamp to 0.6 (ui.lua:132), so 10px text draws at 6px.
  3. No two buttons agree on size. Quick buttons 28/34 (ui.lua:149), tools 60/64 (ui.lua:139), FOLLOW/JOBS 28, 22 or 20 depending on layout (ui.lua:1101-1103, 1153-1155, 1217-1220), and a button's text size depends on its height (ui.lua:617). The bottom bar picks one label size per frame from what fits (ui.lua:1043-1055).
  4. The top bar folds even at 1280×720. All values collapse into one "HUNGRY 0" dropdown and the ten quick buttons (ui.lua:125-128) hide under a gear (ui.lua:735-737): resources and speed are 2+ taps away.
  5. Tools are a mode that sticks. The picked tool stays on and the next tap on the map uses it (main.lua:1666-1690); to pick a morphi after building you go back to SELECT first.
  6. Jobs take a whole screen. JOBS opens two grids of 6-7 buttons, a disliked box and CLOSE (ui.lua:1621-1730): 4-6 taps to change a main and a then-job.
  7. Baby care is a panel inside a panel. TEND, then the panel, FOOD, the food bag, a food, MEADOW (ui.lua:1413-1520, 1524): 5 taps for one meal.
  8. On a phone the bar eats a third of the screen. A 104px info strip plus two rows of 64px tools (ui.lua:155-160), and its hint text is cut.
  9. Speed is one cycling button 1→2→4→1 (main.lua:97, 1357-1362), usually folded under the gear.
  10. The picked-morphi strip is crammed: name, eats, state, two slots, jobs, hunger, mood and a bar in 80px, mostly in the 10px face (ui.lua:1144-1190).
